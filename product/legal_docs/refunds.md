This page is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). The Service is sold to businesses.

## Cancelling

You can cancel a paid plan at any time from your [account page](/account) with "Manage billing", which opens the Stripe billing portal. Cancelling stops future renewals. The portal shows what happens to the period you have already paid for before you confirm. If you cannot use the portal, email {{LEGAL_EMAIL}}.

## Refund window

If you ask within {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of your first payment for a paid plan, we will refund that payment's flat monthly fee in full. This applies once per account.

## What is not refunded

- Flat fees for later renewals, and partial months after the refund window.
- Usage charges for usage that has already been consumed, because the underlying AI and infrastructure costs have been incurred.
- Fees for accounts ended because of a breach of our terms.

## When we always refund

- A charge made in error, or a duplicate charge.
- Usage charged that the Service's own records show did not happen.
- Prepaid fees for a period after we discontinue a paid feature or end your agreement without cause.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails your account moves to the free plan. Stripe retries the payment, and a successful retry restores the paid plan. No refund is due for the time on the free plan.

## How to ask

Email {{LEGAL_EMAIL}} from the address on your account with your account ID or company name and the charge in question. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

## Your legal rights

Nothing here limits any right to a refund or withdrawal that the law gives you and that cannot be waived.
